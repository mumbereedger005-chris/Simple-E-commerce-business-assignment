Our Services

What We Offer

Comprehensive Hardware Solutions

From raw construction materials to specialist tools and expert advice, Mumbere Edger Hardware covers every stage of your building project.

Building Materials Supply

We stock a comprehensive range of construction materials — cement, sand, bricks, iron sheets, roofing tiles, timber, steel bars, and more. All sourced from trusted suppliers.

Cement, Iron Sheets, Timber, Steel Bars.

Hand & Power Tools

A wide selection of professional-grade and DIY tools for every job. Hammers, drills, saws, angle grinders, levels, and measuring equipment — we have it all.

Power Drills, Saws, Grinders, Hand Tools.

Electrical Supplies

Complete electrical fittings for residential and commercial projects. Cables, sockets, switches, circuit breakers, conduits, and lighting fixtures from certified brands.

Cables, Sockets, Breakers, Lighting.

Plumbing & Pipes

Everything for your water and drainage systems — PVC pipes, fittings, valves, water tanks, taps, and sanitary ware for all types of plumbing installations.

PVC Pipes, Fittings, Tanks, Sanitary Ware.

Paints & Finishes

Transform your spaces with our wide range of interior and exterior paints, varnishes, wood stains, and protective coatings. Available in countless colours and finishes.

Interior Paint, Exterior Paint, Varnish, Wood Stain.

Delivery to Site

We deliver heavy materials directly to your construction site or home. Our fleet handles bulk cement, sand, iron sheets, and other large-volume orders safely and on time.

Bulk Orders, Site Delivery, Home Delivery.

Tool Rental

Don't want to buy? Rent professional equipment for your project duration. Concrete mixers, scaffolding, compactors, and more available at affordable daily or weekly rates.

Mixers, Scaffolding, Compactors, Daily / Weekly.

Free Expert Consultation

Our experienced team will help you estimate quantities, choose the right materials, and plan your budget — completely free of charge. Just walk in or call us.

Quantity Survey, Material Selection, Budget Planning.

Safety Equipment

Your safety is our priority. We stock hard hats, safety boots, gloves, reflective vests, goggles, and other personal protective equipment (PPE) for construction sites.

Hard Hats, Safety Boots, Gloves, PPE.
